The Problem

In campus emergencies, time doesn't tick. It bleeds.

WhatsApp forwards, outdated lists, and 3-hour verifications. By the time a donor was found, the window was gone. 63% of college blood requests in UP were delayed due to scattered information.

↳ Average delay: 4.2 hours

02
The Spark — 2:17 AM, Nov 2023

Ananya needed O- . We had 800 contacts. Zero match.

Three friends — a coder, a biotech student, and a volunteer — watched a group chat fail. That night they sketched HemoCampus on a mess bill: verified donors, live stock, one-tap request. No more panic calls.

First donor camp: 17th Dec 2023 • BBD Campus • 104 donors

03
The Solution

A circulatory system for the entire campus.

Like blood flows without you thinking, HemoCampus flows without admin chasing. Auto-verification, expiry alerts, emergency priority queue, and donor privacy first. Built for campus, by campus.

→ Match time: 28 seconds. Not hours.
