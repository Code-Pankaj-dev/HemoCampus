How fast will you respond at 2 AM?

Emergency hotline is human 24x7. Form requests between 12-5 AM get auto-forwarded to 3 nearest O- / AB- donors + on-call volunteer. Avg 28 sec.

Do you charge for blood?

Never. HemoCampus is free for students. We only connect donors. Hospital processing fee is hospital's, not ours. We show it transparently.

Is my phone number safe?

Yes. We never show your number publicly. Request goes via system. Donor approves, then only volunteer shares contact via masked call.

Yes! Select "Camp Collaboration" in form. We bring doctors, kits, certificates. You bring 40+ donors promise. We did 11 camps this year.
