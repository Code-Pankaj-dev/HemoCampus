Welcome to Hemo Campus

Hemo Campus is a modern, cloud-based Blood Bank Management System that simplifies blood donation, donor management, blood requests, and inventory tracking through a secure and user-friendly platform. It connects donors, hospitals, and patients to provide fast, reliable, and efficient blood services whenever needed.

Available Modules

👤

Donor Management

Verify donor details, record vital information, confirm blood group, and maintain secure donor records.

📝

Bleeding Entry & Screening

Record donor bleeding details, perform health screening, and capture test results to ensure a safe and secure blood donation process.

🩸

Blood Transfusion

Manage blood transfusion requests, verify blood compatibility, perform cross-matching, and ensure the safe and timely issue of blood to patients.

🏕️

Blood Camp Management

Organize blood donation camps, register donors, manage camp records, and generate e-Donor Cards for a smooth and efficient donation process.

🔄

Receive & Transfer of Blood

Easily transfer and receive blood units between blood banks and storage centers while maintaining accurate records for safe, efficient, and seamless blood management.

💉

Apheresis

Manage donor selection, prepare blood components, perform quality checks, and maintain accurate records for a safe and efficient apheresis process.
